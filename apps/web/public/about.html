About Zoned Out

Zoned Out is a browser game about housing policy in a stylised Greater Victoria, BC. Under the game is an agent-based model of short-term rentals, long-term rentals, owners and developers, calibrated to the measured effects of BC's 2023 short-term rental law (Bill 35) from an earlier study, bc-str-ban-analysis (dashboard).

What it is not

It is a simplified, calibrated model, not a forecast and not a faithful digital twin of Victoria. It is not policy advice. It is not affiliated with or commissioned by the Government of British Columbia. Effects marked "Assumed" in the game rest on modelling assumptions, not measurements. The real data show that the rule cut short-term rental listings, while its effect on rents was too small to detect.

Privacy

No analytics, no cookies, no accounts and no server: the whole simulation runs in your browser, and the page loads files only from this site. Two things are kept in your browser's local storage, on this device only: whether you skipped the tutorial, and your best score for each city and mandate.

Published neutral policy-rate range, used as a reference interest rate.

Every number and its source is listed in the project's docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md, tagged DATA, LITERATURE, ASSUMED or VERIFY (not yet checked at source).
