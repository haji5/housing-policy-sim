## 3. External validation

- **Held-out Salt Spring Island (H1).** Data: 258 listings in Aug 2023, 252 in Jun 2026 (log change -0.024). Sim (ensemble mean): -0.426 (5-95% -0.661 to -0.196). **Miss.** The model applies the full principal-residence rule from the Nov 2025 opt-in, so Salt Spring's non-PR listings fall as covered areas' did in 2024. The data show almost no fall by Jun 2026 (one snapshot after the opt-in matters most). Plausible reasons outside the model: slower enforcement on the islands, transition time, or hosts already registered as exempt. This is reported, not fitted.
- **Rent bound (S2).** STR-attributable asking-rent change: mean -0.57% (5-95% -1.52% to -0.14%); 60% of the ensemble lies inside the -3.3% to -0.4% bound from the first project.
- **CPI sanity (S1).** Real CPI rented accommodation rose 19.5% (Sep 2023 to Jun 2026); the sim's CPI-style index rose -0.3%. The sim contains no macro rent inflation (rentDriftAnnual = 0): it is built to isolate the STR channel, so it is not expected to match. The STR-attributable part is the S2 number above, which is too small to detect against CPI noise.
